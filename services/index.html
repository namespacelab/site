Common questions

Which service do I need?

Start from the problem. Spreadsheets point to custom software or a CRM, an existing Salesforce setup points to our Salesforce work, repeat tasks point to automation, and system alerts point to AIOps. If you're not sure, describe the problem and we'll tell you.

What's the difference between AI automation and GaaS?

AI automation is a build for one workflow. GaaS is AI we deliver and run for you as an ongoing service, covering both generative AI and AI agents.

What is AIOps?

AI for IT operations. It watches your apps and servers, cuts alert noise, spots trouble early and runs routine fixes you've approved.

Can you do more than one?

Yes. Many projects mix services, like a custom CRM with automation built in.

What does it cost?

It depends on the problem, so we price it once we understand it.

Not sure which one you need?

Tell us what's slowing your team down. We'll tell you honestly what would help, even if it's something small.
