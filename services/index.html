What's the difference between AI automation and GaaS?

AI automation is a build for one workflow. GaaS is AI delivered and run as an ongoing service, covering both generative AI and AI agents. (Monthly plan wording: details to follow.)

What is AIOps?

AI for IT operations. It watches your apps and servers, cuts alert noise, spots trouble early and runs approved routine fixes.
