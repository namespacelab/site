How we work

We can start with one small fix. You see it working before committing to anything bigger. Every change is built and tested in a sandbox. Nothing reaches your live org without your approval.

PlanDesignBuildTestSupport

  1. PlanWe learn how your team uses Salesforce today and agree on the smallest build that fixes the real problem.
  2. DesignYou see the proposed changes — fields, screens and flows — before we build, and nothing moves forward until it makes sense to you.
  3. BuildWe build in a sandbox, in small working pieces, so you see real progress early.
  4. TestWe check the work against your real tasks in the sandbox before anyone relies on it.
  5. SupportAfter it goes live we stay on to fix, adjust and add what you need next.

FAQ

Common questions

What can you do with an existing Salesforce org?

We can clean up data and setup, add custom features, automate manual steps and support your users, so you get more from what you already pay for.

Can we start with one small fix?

Yes. Common first jobs are tidying fields and layouts, automating one manual step, or building one screen people actually like using. You see it working before committing to anything bigger.

Will you change our live org without asking?

No. Every change is built and tested in a sandbox. Nothing reaches your live org without your approval.

Can you connect Salesforce to the other tools we use?

Often, yes. We connect to the tools you already use where they allow it, and if one doesn't, we'll say so before we build.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we price it once we understand how your org works today.

What's Salesforce not doing for you?

Tell us in a few lines. We'll tell you honestly what would help, even if it's something small.
