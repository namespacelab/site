FAQ

What is GaaS?

GaaS means Generative or Agentic AI as a Service: a provider builds and runs AI that drafts, answers and completes tasks, so you pay for work getting done rather than software your team has to operate.

How is GaaS different from SaaS?

SaaS gives your team software to use. GaaS gives you AI that does part of the work inside that software, with people reviewing results.

Does GaaS replace our current software?

No. Your CRM and tools stay where records live; the AI works across them.

Is our data used to train public models?

We set it up so your data stays private to your business.
