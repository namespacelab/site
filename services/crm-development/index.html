FAQ

Common questions

What is a custom CRM?

A customer relationship system built around how you sell, holding leads, clients and follow-ups in one place, without features you don't use.

Should we build a custom CRM or use Salesforce?

A custom CRM is a good fit when your process is unusual, your team is small, and you want only what you use. Salesforce is a better fit when you already pay for it, or you need its wider set of tools. We do both and will tell you honestly which is the better starting point.

Can it connect to our email and website forms?

Often, yes. We connect to the tools you already use where they allow it, and if one doesn't, we'll say so before we build.

Can you move our data from spreadsheets?

Yes. We can bring your spreadsheets and contacts across, cleaned up, and check it with you before you start using it.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we price it once we understand how you work today.

Where do your leads get lost?

Tell us in a few lines. We'll tell you honestly what would help, even if it's something small.
