Follow one lead, before and after

Before: an enquiry lands in one inbox, gets forwarded, and goes quiet. After: it's logged, assigned, reminded and tracked to a yes or no.

What goes in it

Leads and pipeline · clients and contacts · tasks and reminders · emails and notes on each record · simple reports · roles so people see what they need.

Custom CRM or Salesforce?

Custom when your process is unusual, your team is small and you want only what you use. Salesforce when you need its wide ecosystem or already pay for it. → Salesforce

Moving your data
