Custom CRM or Salesforce?

  • Custom CRM: your process is unusual, your team is small to mid-size, you want no per-user licence lock-in.
  • Salesforce: you need a large ecosystem, many apps and room for many teams. We do both and will recommend honestly.

Not the right fit

If a standard CRM fits your sales process as it is, use it. We can help set it up.
