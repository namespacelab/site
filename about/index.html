Why "Namespace"

In code, a namespace gives every name its own place, so nothing clashes and nothing gets lost. Two things can share a name and still never get mixed up.

That's what we do for a business. Leads, jobs, files, approvals and answers each get a clear name and a clear place. When everything has a place, people stop hunting and start working.

What we believe

  • Start with the problem. The tool comes second. Sometimes the answer is a small fix, not a big build.
  • Plain words beat clever ones. If we can't explain it simply, we don't understand it well enough yet.
  • People stay in charge. Automation and AI handle the repeat work. A person approves what matters.

What we won't do

  • Post fake reviews, made-up numbers or borrowed logos.
  • Start building before we understand how your work actually runs.
  • Lock you into something you can't explain to the next person. (Code ownership wording: details to follow.)
  • Sell you a big project when a small one will do.

How we work

One team plans, designs, builds, tests and supports your project, so you never have to re-explain it. You'll see progress in small, working pieces, not one big reveal at the end. ("You'll talk to the people who build it": details to follow.)

Who is Namespace Lab?

Namespace Lab (namespacelab.com) is a software development company. We build custom software, web and mobile apps, CRM and Salesforce solutions, and AI apps and automation for businesses and nonprofits. We are not Namespace Labs, namespace.so, or a developer-infrastructure or cloud-compute platform.

Why the name?

A namespace keeps every name in its own place without clashing. We do the same for your business data and work.

Do you have past client work to show?
