About Namespace Lab

Every part of your work, in its place.

We're a software company that gives your work a clear shape, then builds the software around it.

See if we'd work well together→See what we build

Why we're called Namespace

In code, a namespace gives every name its own place, so nothing clashes and nothing gets lost.

We do the same for a business. Leads, jobs, files, approvals and answers each get a clear place, so people stop hunting and start working.
