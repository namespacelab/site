A day in admissions season

8 am: a new enquiry. 10 am: an application, missing two documents. 1 pm: someone emails to ask for them, again. 4 pm: three parents call to ask where things stand. Tomorrow, the same.

Where time leaks

Chasing missing documents · answering "where's my application?" · copying data between forms, sheets and the learning platform · building reports for leadership.

What it can look like

*Typical use cases, not client work.*

  • Admissions CRM: every applicant, document and follow-up in one place, with automatic reminders.
  • Parent portal: status, forms and messages in one login.
  • Learning-platform link: enrolled students flow into your LMS without retyping.
  • Salesforce Education Cloud: Details to follow.
  • AI help desk: answers course and admissions questions from your own documents.

Student data, handled carefully

Common questions

Short answers. Ask us anything else on a call.

Can an admissions CRM connect to our learning platform?

Usually, yes. Most learning platforms allow data in and out, so enrolled students can move across without retyping.

Do we need Salesforce?

No. A custom CRM can work well for smaller schools; Salesforce suits those already on it or needing its ecosystem.

Can parents see application status themselves?

Yes, through a simple portal, which cuts the "where are we?" calls.
