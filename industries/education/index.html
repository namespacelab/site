Software for education

Portals, admissions CRMs and Education Cloud work for schools and colleges.

Get in touch

Namespace Lab builds software for schools, colleges and training providers: student and parent portals, admissions and enquiry CRMs, scheduling tools and integrations with learning platforms. For teams on Salesforce, we configure and customise Education Cloud. The aim is simple: less time on admin, faster answers for students and families.

What we build for education

  • Admissions CRM that tracks every enquiry from first contact to enrolment.
  • Student and parent portal for timetables, documents, messages and payments.
  • AI assistant that answers common admissions questions from your own policies.

Common questions

Short answers. Ask us anything else on a call.

Can you connect to our learning platform?

Usually, yes. Most learning platforms offer an API or data export. We confirm what your platform and licence allow during discovery, before we quote.

Do you work with Salesforce Education Cloud?

Yes, for configuration and customisation: objects, Flows, Apex and LWC. We don't resell licences.

How do you handle student data?

We collect only the student data the software needs, limit access by role, and use it only for the purposes your school authorises: never for marketing. Your school stays in control of its records, and we build to support the privacy laws and policies you follow.
