A day in dispatch

The phone rings all morning with bookings. Jobs go on the whiteboard. Drivers call in to ask what's next. Invoices get typed up at night.

Where time leaks

Phone bookings · manual dispatch · drivers calling in · missed pickups and complaints · late invoicing.

What it can look like

*Typical use cases, not client work.*

  • Online booking: customers book and pay for a pickup or bin themselves.
  • Dispatch board: jobs assigned to drivers and routes on screen, not a whiteboard.
  • Driver app: today's jobs, photos of the emptied bin, offline sync.
  • Customer portal: bookings, invoices and service requests in one login.
