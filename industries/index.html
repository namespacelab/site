FAQ

Common questions

Which industries does Namespace Lab build software for?

Mainly education, nonprofits, real estate, hospitality, engineering firms and solid waste management, plus others with similar problems.

Do you only work with these six?

No. They're where we focus, not a fence. If your team deals with manual admin, slow handoffs or enquiries slipping through the cracks, we can still help.

Is the software different per sector?

The building blocks are the same. The workflow, wording and screens are shaped to the sector, so a real estate CRM and a donor CRM feel like they were made for that team.

What do you build for these industries?

Custom software and CRMs, web and mobile apps, Salesforce setups and custom code, and AI apps and automation. We also help turn ideas into SaaS products, or deliver and run AI for you as a service (GaaS).

Do we need a big project to start?

No. You can start with one piece, like lead follow-up or a single workflow, as a small pilot. You see it running before committing to anything bigger.

Don't see your industry?

If your team repeats work, chases approvals or loses leads, the fix probably looks similar. Tell us where your time goes, and we'll tell you honestly what would help, even if it's something small.
