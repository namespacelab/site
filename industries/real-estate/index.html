Real estate

No enquiry should wait until Monday.

Software that catches every enquiry, gets it to the right agent, and keeps the follow-up going.

Show us how leads reach you today→See what we build

Where leads slip away

  • Too many inboxes.

    Enquiries arrive from portals, messaging apps, calls, email and the website, and nobody sees all of them.

  • No clear owner.

    A lead comes in, everyone assumes someone else has it, and it goes cold.

  • After-hours gaps.

    A buyer asks on Saturday evening. Nobody replies until Monday, and by then they've booked a viewing elsewhere.

  • Notes everywhere.

    Viewing notes, client preferences and follow-ups sit in notebooks, phones and spreadsheets.

What changes

Before: Saturday, 7 pm. A buyer asks about a listing on a portal. It sits in a shared inbox until Monday.

After: The enquiry is logged and sent to the agent on duty. The buyer gets a friendly first reply in minutes with a few viewing slots, and the agent takes it from there.

Common questions

Yes. It can send a quick first reply, answer basic questions from your listings and offer viewing times, then hand the lead to an agent. A person stays in charge of the conversation.

Often, yes. We connect to the tools you already use where they allow it, and if one doesn't, we'll say so before we build.
