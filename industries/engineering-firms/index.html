A day on a project

Revision C of a drawing goes to three reviewers by email. One replies to the wrong version. A site report is still on paper in someone's van. Timesheets are due Friday.

Where time leaks

Chasing approvals · version mix-ups · retyping site reports · timesheets and hours matched to projects by hand.

Common questions

Short answers. Ask us anything else on a call.

How can an engineering firm automate drawing approvals?

Route each revision to the right reviewers automatically, track who approved what, and keep one clear latest version, instead of emailing files.

Can field reports work offline?

Yes. The app saves on the device and syncs when there's signal.

Do we have to replace our CAD software?

No. The system tracks approvals around your files, not instead of your design tools.
