Common questions

Short answers. Ask us anything else on a call.

Can you automate document approvals?

Yes, with routing, reminders and a full approval history.

Can you connect to our CAD or accounting tools?

Often, through the tool's API, plug-in options or file exports (such as drawing lists, PDFs or spreadsheets). We confirm what your CAD and accounting tools support during discovery.

Can you build a system to track projects, drawings and approvals?

Yes. We build project tools for mechanical and civil firms that track each project's tasks, drawing revisions, approvals and deadlines in one place, with automatic alerts when something is overdue. Where your CAD or accounting tools allow it, we connect them through their API or file exports.
